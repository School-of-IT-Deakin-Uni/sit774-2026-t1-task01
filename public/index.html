Choosing the Right Footwear

Comfortable, supportive footwear reduces fatigue and helps prevent injury. Look for good grip and cushioning.

Quick Tips

  • Try shoes at the end of the day
  • Test on uneven ground if possible
  • Pair with suitable socks
